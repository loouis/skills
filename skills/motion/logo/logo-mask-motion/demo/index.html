Logo mask motion: five leaves

Each petal is a whole leaf with a line all the way round. Its mask is a window with a copy of the petal in front cut out. The mask stays still while the petal swings out, so the petal comes out flush. When the petal starts to bounce back, the mask grows above the waist and the gap opens.

Lines under lines: the same model on a ring-and-spokes mark

Open lines instead of closed shapes, and no gaps anywhere. Each spoke is extended straight along its own line past both ends, so its ends sit under the rings however it moves. Its mask is the outer ring filled, as the window, with both rings' strokes cut out a twentieth of a stroke narrower than they are painted. Nothing grows on landing.
