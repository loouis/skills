# Film Skills

Skills for making finished short films in code: showreels, product films, launch stories and explainers that are designed, animated, scored, rendered and reviewed on one frame-based timeline.

<!-- skills:start -->
| Skill | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| [Motion Director Film](motion-director-film) | Direct, animate, score and QA a finished short film in code |
<!-- skills:end -->

## Use a skill

Copy the complete skill folder into your agent's skills directory, or run `python3 install.py` from the repo root for Claude Code. Then fill in the brief, or as much of it as you know, and hand it over:

```text
Use $motion-director-film to make a 15 s 16:9 product film for this app from the live product, music and sfx, unattended, ending on the name and URL.
```

In Claude Code you can also just describe it ("make a 30 second launch video for this product, rendered in code") or type `/motion-director-film`.
