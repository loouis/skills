# Layout, architecture and outdoor living

## Plan a home before a picture

Evaluate arrival with groceries, storage, cooking, serving, dining, conversation, access to the terrace, privacy, cleaning and furniture movement. Mark where people stand, sit and open things. Trace the everyday route outside the cook's work aisle and occupied stool zone. A camera cannot certify clearance.

In the historical comparison of 17 plans across six developments, the useful lessons were:

- Burly 1401: separate cooking, dining and lounge zones with usable outdoor sitting/dining. Broad social space was a strong fit for the brief; undimensioned clearances still required checking.
- Ocean House three-bedroom dual aspect: separate day/night zones and place service/prep rooms between them. Long internal routes consume area; dual aspect alone does not prove ventilation.
- Burly 2102: a private bedroom wing and second retreat support different daily activities.
- MIRA: concealed pantry/appliance integration keeps the show kitchen calm.
- Bayview compact two-bedroom: a proper foyer, bedroom corridor and wall kitchen can work better than a forced island.
- Smaller Burly layouts: preserve a dining table and usable terrace rather than making stools the only eating place.

These were design judgements within household/size groups, not universal rankings or investment advice. Marketing area schedules sometimes conflicted with prose; retain discrepancies. Published layouts are not as-built surveys.

## Starting dimensions and audit method

| Element | Starting proposal | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Living zone | 9.40 × 7.80 m | Fits supplied shell, dining and service access |
| Island | 3.20 × 1.05 m; 900 mm finished height | Supports, carcass, knee space, sink and services |
| Stone top and end panels | 70 mm each | World-space envelope, horizontal top, joint easing |
| Work aisle | 1250 mm counter to counter | Local hardware projections and appliance operation |
| Main route | 1500 mm beside island/behind sofa in the example | Occupied furniture and continuous route, not just one gap |
| Slider | 1200 mm intended clear opening | Actual moving leaves, handle, stack and curtain interference |
| Seating recess | 400 mm example | Knees, footrest, carcass and structural support |
| Sofa-to-table | About 450 mm rigid reach gap | Welts, cushions, throw and fringe intrusions separately |
| Terrace | About 2.80 m depth | Seating use, drainage, rails, approach and door opening |

All are design allowances. Do not label them legal minima or accessible/compliant dimensions. Verify current jurisdictional requirements and supplier information when the task includes construction or specification. The historical NKBA work-aisle reference is a useful planning comparison, not Australian approval.

Use evaluated world-space geometry, not only object origins or nominal dimension labels. Record axes, measured objects, projections, tolerances and scope. Include door/drawer envelopes in a dimensioned plan and save top/working-side evidence. Static AABBs are conservative and can miss shape-specific conflicts; follow with section/ray/mesh checks where the shape matters.

A developed example had a 1249.65 mm counter gap but only 1158.85 mm local closed-hardware gap. Its 449.49 mm rigid sofa/welt-to-table gap became about 309 mm with all textile projections. Report both. A simplified open oven panel left only 613 mm; it did not establish manufacturer operating clearance, ventilation or safe concurrent use. Do not call this resolved because the closed-door hero looks spacious.

## Architecture that affects realism

Model exterior slab/soffit, wall thickness, glazing recesses, columns and door frames before lighting. Removing an overhang to make the interior brighter changes the architecture. Hallway doors need jambs, stops/rebates, hinge or concealed-pivot logic, hardware and consistent reveals. A concealed frame is a construction choice, not a blank painted rectangle.

Plan floor plank widths and tile modules. Continue intended joint lines through visible thresholds where plausible, but represent the external paving assembly separately. Avoid a single slab texture over a field of supposedly individual tiles. Small bevels and seal lines need scale; huge dark gaps read as poor fitting.

## Balcony options and their tradeoffs

The researched options below are a shortlist, not selections or engineered assemblies. Recheck dimensions and finishes on official pages before modelling a chosen variant.

| Family | Researched options | Design decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sliding doors | Alspec ProGlide Minimalist/UltraFlat; AWS SlideMASTER 704 | Sightline, panel stacking, actual threshold/drainage and coastal exposure |
| Glass rail | Axiom Paramount, ALLOY MaxVista, Axiom Neo | Base-supported versus semi-framed, top cap, visible shoes/posts and continuous returns |
| Glass appearance | Viridian SuperClear reference | Edge tint/transmission; exact laminated/structural build-up remains a specification task |
| Finish | Dulux Electro family | Warm champagne direction, sample-confirmed colour and application suitability |
| Interior jamb | EzyJamb ISD reference | Flush appearance with real frame/rebate geometry |
| Paving | Dekton Nacre; Atlas Concorde Boost Mineral | Exterior-rated texture/module, slip performance and installation requirements need verification |
| Outdoor light | Flos Camouflage example | Discreet warm illumination, not a row of glowing ornaments |

Furniture shortlist: Tribù Elio has a warm shaped teak frame and woven shell; Nodi is lighter and more open; RODA HARP 368 is a more compact graphic cord/metal option. Historical listed envelopes were Elio 670×760×800 mm, Nodi 600×720×710 mm, HARP 630×650×710 mm. Seat-height figures differ with cushions. Elio plus a Dunes low table was the recommended warm direction, not a user-approved product selection. Drops was another table reference.

Place chairs so their backs look good from inside and seated users retain the outlook. Test occupied pull-out space, a route to the rail, door operation and cleaning. Proposed 1200 mm entry/900 mm rear routes were unverified allowances; remeasure after selection. Keep cushions modest and specify outdoor materials rather than simply moving indoor upholstery outside.

Show terrace depth with paving, threshold, glass support, front rail and both visible returns. Model each pane with thickness and support; do not let a front rail terminate in empty space. Glass/rail sizes, loading, waterproofing, drain and slab set-down must not be claimed engineered from a visual concept. The shoreline, elevated view and background coverage are handled in [lighting/camera](07-lighting-reflections-camera.md).
