# Provenance and portability

## What this package contains

An original procedural workflow distilled from a multi-stage coastal interior study, public source links, generic helper scripts, JSON input examples and an original small material vignette. It contains no source-project scene, manufacturer mesh, downloaded reference photograph, production render, local workstation path or private asset manifest.

The public names Burly, MIRA and the other developers/manufacturers identify reference sources. They do not imply affiliation, approval, shared assets or exact replication. The visual observations and historical product details were recorded in October 2026; revisit official pages for current models, dimensions, finishes, availability or construction claims.

## Rights and truthfulness

- Reference images remain their creators' work. Inspect them as references and retain attribution in the receiving project. Do not publish them or use them as output textures merely because they are downloadable.
- Manufacturer architectural assets can be useful but are not automatically CC0. Keep archives/licences locally and distinguish factory geometry from custom modifications/finishes.
- The studied Muuto assets were manufacturer downloads; the selected Poliform stool was a custom photo/dimension reconstruction. Do not relabel either as the other.
- Poly Haven and ambientCG sources used in the studied recipe had CC0 licensing. Verify at retrieval, retain the exact source/version and distinguish procedural textures from scans.
- Book covers, branded appliances and plant assets need their own source records. Use original/public-domain cover art in a public demo.
- Generated background images must retain generator/prompt/provenance and be labelled illustrative when not a verified outlook.
- Inspect embedded image metadata before publication. Blender can include the full scene path even when visible stamps are off; suppress filename/hostname metadata in the public render path and verify exported files.
- Do not infer whether marketing pictures are AI, CGI, photography or a composite from polish alone. Prefer creator credits and process evidence; otherwise say it is unverified.

## What the skill does not claim

It does not include an exact recoverable production scene or every external asset. Exact replay requires that scene, asset versions and records in the receiving project. The numerical recipe is contextual guidance; the portable demo is an independent procedural study. Neither a scripted render nor a review score certifies structural design, product fidelity, buildability, code compliance or reference parity.

The whole-room benchmark remained unmet in the source review. Preserve that boundary while using the workflow to build better new scenes. The goal is to avoid repeated mistakes and make progress inspectable, not to automate a flattering score.
