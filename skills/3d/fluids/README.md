# 3D Fluids

Blender water skills: FLIP water sheets with bubbles, clear studio water and droplets, product water splashes, and sunlit pools with floor caustics and underwater sun rays. Simulation, rendering and playback recipes for fluid effects. Distinguish simulated liquid from added bubble geometry, and distinguish source renders from display or product integration.
