# Transfer to motion only after the still works

Preserve the approved contact and reflection across the entire clip. A field that looks right initially can become mirror-like or banded at the end. Inspect every native reflection frame plus first/middle/last full compositions, same-render raw checkpoints and normal/quarter-speed playback. Review at least two repetitions before claiming a seamless loop.

For explicit three-plate animation, render all three branches at identical times, camera, exposure, resolution and seed policy. Use actual matching image sequences in the saved compositor and reopen it to reproduce first/middle/last frames. A packed still substituted into an animated composition is not a validated movie. The contact recipe's row-based gain is tied to its fixed camera; regenerate/revalidate its placement when the camera or product moves.

Use smooth time-dependent phase and amplitude; avoid randomizing vertices independently per frame. Track the first reflected letter, lower distortion, attached contour, ripple crest and open-water smoothness separately. Keep local deformation coordinates distinct from world-space falling drops and impacts.

For runoff, keep the sheet root attached to the product while the free edge drains; use independent actions for independent beads. Do not make attached beads jump to a world-space path. Once upper runoff is removed, remove its falling drops and impact packets too while preserving explicitly retained condensation.

The included contact field evaluates continuous time, but its latest selected appearance was validated as a still. Numerical continuity tests do not prove good motion, buoyancy, entrainment, coalescence, breakup, surface tension or volume conservation. Test those separately if a fluid simulation is requested; do not rename prescribed motion as coupled CFD.
